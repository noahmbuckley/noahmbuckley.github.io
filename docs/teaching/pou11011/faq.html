FAQ

POU11011 — Introduction to Political Science, Michaelmas Term 2026

Read this before you email your TA or me — most questions land here. If your question isn’t answered below, start with your TA (see contacting us in the module outline).

Extensions

Extensions are not granted by TAs — they go through the module coordinator (me), via your College Tutor. See the syllabus’s late work section for the full policy: get in touch before the deadline, not after or a few hours before it. If you have a LENS accommodation, you get an automatic one-week extension without needing to ask. Nothing is accepted once work has been marked and returned to other students, or after the end of teaching term.

[TBD: Microsoft Form URL] — an intake form for extension requests is planned; until it exists, requests go through your College Tutor as described above.

I missed a tutorial

Come to a different group that week if one has space — ask your TA, who can check with the others. Otherwise, you can still submit that week’s discussion points for credit, at or before the next tutorial meets. A single missed session is not a crisis; go to your TA first.

I can’t access a reading

Nine times out of ten this is a library login problem: go to the Library site, sign in, then follow the link from Blackboard. If it still won’t open, tell your TA — if it’s a genuine broken link, they’ll flag it to be fixed for everyone, and you won’t be penalised for it at that week’s tutorial.

My tutorial group or timetable clashes

Tutorial allocation is handled by the department, not by me or your TA — contact the school office to sort out a clash or swap.

I think my mark is wrong

Read the rubric first and check which band your comment points to. Then go to your TA — that conversation, with the descriptors in hand, resolves most of these. Only come to me if you’ve already done that and still disagree; there’s a fixed window of 10 working days after marks are released to raise a query.

Can I use ChatGPT / Claude / other GenAI tools?

It depends on the assessment — the syllabus sets out exactly what’s permitted for each of the three components, using the same categories as the School of Social Sciences and Philosophy Assignment Submission Form, which must be attached as page 1 of every written submission. Whatever you use, tick the matching box — disclosing a permitted use costs you nothing. Don’t ask a model for references: it invents them.

Is there an exam?

No. This module has no final examination. Assessment is discussion points and tutorials (10%), the short blog-post assignment (30%), and the final essay (60%). See assessment in the syllabus.

Are lectures recorded?

Some lectures may be recorded, but don’t count on it — availability isn’t automatic. Slides, not recordings, are what you should rely on for review.

Where do I find the slides?

Slides are posted on the course website after each lecture, and a student PDF version is also on Blackboard, both before and after each lecture.

Who do I email?

Start with your own TA — anything about content, readings, tutorials, discussion points, or how an assignment works. Come to me only for what a TA can’t settle: extensions, serious personal difficulty, a mark you still think is wrong after talking to your TA, or a question about the module as a whole — and copy your TA when you do. See contacting us for the full version.

My discussion point was rejected — what now?

Your TA can reject a submission that’s too short or doesn’t genuinely engage with the reading. There are three submissions across the term and only two need to pass for the maximum mark on this component, so one rejection isn’t fatal. Ask your TA what specifically was missing before the next one is due.

Word counts and formatting

State your word count at the top of every piece. Going over the limit costs three per cent. Submissions go to Blackboard in Word or PDF with the Assignment Submission Form as page 1 — a submission without it isn’t accepted. Full formatting requirements are in the syllabus’s written work: format requirements section.
